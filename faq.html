Frequently Asked Questions

What is UniVibe?
UniVibe is your all-in-one platform for discovering, joining, and managing university events and clubs. Stay connected and never miss out on campus life!
How do I join an event?
Simply click the "Join Now" button on any event card. You'll be guided through the registration process or redirected to the event's details page.
Can I add my own club or event?
Yes! Use the "Contact Us" page to submit your club or event. Our team will review your submission and get in touch with you for verification.
Is UniVibe free to use?
Absolutely! UniVibe is free for all students and clubs. Enjoy all features without any charges.
How do I contact support?
You can reach out to us anytime via the "Contact Us" page. We aim to respond to all queries within 24 hours.
